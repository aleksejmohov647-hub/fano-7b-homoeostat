--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This matches the atmospheric mass-squared splitting measured in oscillation
experiments:
  sqrt(Delta m_31^2)_exp ~= sqrt(2.453 x 10^-3 eV^2) ~= 0.0495 eV.

Assuming normal ordering (m_1 << m_2 < m_3) with solar splitting

well below the Planck/DESI upper bound (< 0.072 - 0.12 eV) and directly
testable by Euclid and CMB-S4.

4. GEOMETRIC DERIVATION OF THE KOIDE RELATION Q = 2/3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For charged leptons (e, mu, tau), the empirical Koide ratio is:
  Q = (m_e + m_mu + m_tau) / (sqrt(m_e) + sqrt(m_mu) + sqrt(m_tau))^2 = 2/3
(experimental value Q_exp = 0.6666605 +/- 0.0000067).

In the C_3-symmetric circulant mass basis on the 3-point generation orbit
O_3 = l_0, the square-root mass eigenvalues take the Brannen-Koide form:
  sqrt(m_k) = v_0 * (1 + r * cos(theta_0 + 2*pi*k/3)),   k = 0, 1, 2,
where the constant term 1 projects onto the S_3-invariant vacuum line vector
e_0 = (1, 1, 1)^T / sqrt(3), and the oscillatory term projects onto the
2-dimensional orthogonal doublet space spanned by the 4-point affine plane
AG(2,2) = PG(2,2) \ l_0.

In R^3, the Koide ratio evaluates to:
  Q = (3 + (3/2) * r^2) / 9 = (1 + r^2 / 2) / 3.

Equipartition of quadratic norm between the S_3-singlet direction (along l_0)
and the C_3-doublet plane (orthogonal to l_0) fixes the amplitude to r = sqrt(2)
(corresponding to a 45-degree angle between (sqrt(m_e), sqrt(m_mu), sqrt(m_tau))
and (1, 1, 1)), yielding the exact rational value:
  Q = (1 + 2/2) / 3 = 2/3.
