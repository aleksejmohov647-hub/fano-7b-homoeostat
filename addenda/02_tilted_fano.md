$$\mathbf{\hat{\mathcal{W}}}_{\text{ord}} = \begin{pmatrix} 3(4!) \\ 4(3!) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 72 \\ 24 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{\hat{\mathcal{W}}}_{\text{dis}} = \begin{pmatrix} 3(!4) \\ 4(!3) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 27 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}$$

Where:
* $| \mathcal{S}_3 \times \mathcal{S}_4 | = (3!) \times (4!) = 6 \times 24 = 144$
* The structural displacement $\Delta$ evaluates directly to:
  $$\Delta = 3(!4) - 4(3!) = 27 - 24 = 3$$
* The regular defect $\delta_{\text{reg}}$ evaluates to:
  $$\delta_{\text{reg}} = 4(!3) + \Delta = 8 + 3 = 11$$
